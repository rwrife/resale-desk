Resale Desk — Offline resale-preparation workspace

Tidying up? Keep every sale organized before you post, pack, and ship. Resale Desk is a private, on-device workspace for documenting what you are selling, drafting honest listings, and keeping clean records of every parcel — without tying your notes, photos, and prices to any marketplace account.

CONDITION YOU CAN STAND BEHIND
- Build your own condition rubrics or start from built-in templates for apparel, electronics, books, and more.
- Answer each check pass, fail, or unknown and attach defect photos to the item.
- Resale Desk only suggests a grade when every required check is answered — gaps stay honestly unknown instead of being guessed.

STAGE LISTINGS YOUR WAY
- Draft titles and descriptions in your own words with personal phrasing templates.
- Track asking and minimum prices with full price history, then copy the finished text to paste anywhere.

PACK AND SHIP WITH A CHECKLIST
- Group items into a parcel and work through a packing checklist so nothing is skipped.
- Record carrier and tracking numbers yourself; Resale Desk stores them without contacting any carrier.

HONEST RECORDS, TRULY YOURS
- An append-only ledger of listings, price changes, shipments, and outcomes builds your season totals from recorded amounts only.
- Export items, drafts, parcels, and outcomes to CSV, or take a versioned JSON backup and restore it with a full preview first.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account. No cloud. No ads. No trackers. Resale Desk works entirely offline and never uploads your photos or prices.
- Camera and photo access happen only when you choose to capture or import, and exports are always user-initiated.
- Designed with VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type, and large one-thumb controls for quick capture at the pile of stuff.

Resale Desk prepares the sale; you post it wherever you like. Resale Desk never connects to marketplaces, buys shipping labels, handles payments, or appraises items.
